# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, constructive, and specific. Discuss ideas and behavior rather than attacking people. Welcome contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Never use project channels to expose personal, customer, company, or credential data.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, threats, deliberate privacy violations, and sustained disruption of project work.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through GitHub. The maintainer may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect people or the project.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible. Good-faith reports will not result in retaliation.
